Privacy policy

The Gift · RNK Studios · updated October 2026

The Gift is a free Bible app with optional paid plans. We collect as little as we can, we never sell or rent your data, and there are no ads, trackers or analytics in the app.

Without an account

You can read, search, follow a plan and keep a journal without signing up. That all stays in your browser on your device. We only see ordinary web requests (which chapter to load), and we don't keep request logs by default.

With an account

  • Account: your username, display name and password. The password is stored only as a salted scrypt hash.
  • Profile (optional): a picture, a short bio and a favourite verse. You can hide your reading progress and rewards from your profile.
  • Bible progress: which chapters you mark as read, and on which day (for streaks and rewards).
  • Groups and friends: the groups you join, your role, your messages, verses you share, group reading-plan ticks, and your friends and friend requests.
  • Uploads: pictures and, for approved artists, songs. Pictures are resized on your device first, which also removes their location data.
  • Safety records: reports you make, people you block, and a security log of sign-ins, password changes and admin actions (with IP address and device type, such as “Chrome on Android”). Security logs are kept for 180 days.
  • Signed-in devices: for each session, when it started, when it was last used and the device type. You can see and end them under Me → Devices & security.

Sync & backup (Plus and Premium)

If you use sync, your highlights, bookmarks and reading plan are stored on our server. Your journal is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded, with a key made from your password. We store only the encrypted version and cannot read it. If your password is reset by an admin, the old backup can't be decrypted any more.

Payments

Card payments on the web are handled by Stripe; payments in the Android app are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details. We keep your plan, its status and renewal date, and the reference the payment provider gives us, so we can confirm your plan with them.

Finding a church

When you search for churches, your location is rounded to about one kilometre on our server, and only that rounded position (or the town name you type) is sent to OpenStreetMap's public services (Overpass and Nominatim). Your browser never contacts them, and we don't store your location.

Church enquiries

If you use the “For churches” form, we keep the contact details you give so we can reply.

Who can see what

  • Group messages: only members of that group.
  • Your profile: other signed-in people who open it (progress and rewards only if you allow it).
  • Approved artist pages and songs: everyone.
  • Admins can see reports, the security log and what's needed to review artists and songs.

Where it's kept

On our own server, reached through Cloudflare's network (which provides the encrypted connection). Connections are always encrypted, and every request is checked against your session.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app (Me → Settings → Delete account), or see how to delete your account. It removes your account, profile, messages, friends, progress, uploads, artist profile and sync backup straight away. Payment providers keep their own records as the law requires. Security-log lines keep only a numeric reference, for up to 180 days.

Children

Accounts, groups and chat are for people aged 13 and over. Reading the Bible needs no account.
