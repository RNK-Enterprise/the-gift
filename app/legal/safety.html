Community & child safety standards

The Gift · RNK Studios · updated October 2026

The Gift is a place to read the Bible and grow together. These standards apply to everything people post: messages, profiles, pictures, songs and artist pages.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging violence or self-harm
  • Hate or discrimination against people for who they are
  • Sexual content of any kind
  • Spam, scams, or impersonating someone
  • Sharing someone's private information
  • Uploading music or pictures you don't have the rights to

Child safety

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any sexualisation of minors. Such content is removed and the accounts involved are suspended. Where the law requires, or where a child may be at risk, we report it to the relevant authorities (such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, NCMEC) and preserve the evidence for them.

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Groups are private and joined only by invite link.

How to report

  • In a group chat, tap Report under any message.
  • On someone's profile, tap Report or block.
  • Or email [contact email].

Reports go to the site admins, never to the person reported. Blocking someone hides their messages from you and stops their friend requests.

What we do

Admins review reports and can delete content and suspend accounts. Group leaders and moderators can remove messages and members in their groups. Every artist and song is reviewed before it goes live. Moderation actions are recorded in a security log.
