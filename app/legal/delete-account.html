Delete your account

The Gift · RNK Studios · updated October 2026

You can delete your The Gift account and its data yourself, at any time.

  1. Open The Gift (in the app, or in any browser) and sign in.
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Type your password and confirm.

What is deleted, straight away

  • Your account, username, name, picture, bio and favourite verse
  • Your messages, shared verses, friendships and friend requests, and blocks
  • Your Bible progress, rewards and group plan ticks
  • Your uploads, artist profile and songs
  • Your sync backup (including the encrypted journal) and every signed-in session

Groups you lead pass to another member. Your journal and highlights stored on your own device stay there until you clear them.

What may remain

  • Stripe or Google Play keep their own payment records, as the law requires. Cancel any subscription there as well; deleting your account here doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription by itself.
  • Security-log lines keep a numeric reference for up to 180 days.
  • Reports others made about your content keep a copy of what was reported, for safety reviews.

Can't sign in?

Email [contact email] from an address you can verify and tell us your username, and we'll help.
