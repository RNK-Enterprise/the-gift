# Music

## Hymns/

22 classic hymns for the app's Music section: the words (`hymns.json`) and a recording of each
(`audio/*.mp3`).

**Words.** Every text is in the public domain: each author died long ago (the most recent was
Fanny Crosby, in 1915). The texts were set by hand from their standard published forms,
proofread against public-domain hymnal transcriptions on Wikisource (chiefly *The Army and Navy
Hymnal*, 1920, and Newton's *Olney Hymns*, 1779). Some well-loved hymns are **not** here
because their words are still under copyright somewhere, e.g. *How Great Thou Art* (1949) and
*Great Is Thy Faithfulness* (1923, public domain in the US but not in the UK or EU).

**Recordings.** From Wikimedia Commons, downloaded as the MP3 versions Commons provides
(format conversion only). Most are volunteer recordings from *The Army and Navy Hymnal* set,
released into the public domain; *Amazing Grace* is a US Air Force Reserve Band performance
(public domain as US government work); *Rock of Ages* is a 1913 Edison recording (public
domain). One recording is openly licensed, not public domain: *What a Friend We Have in Jesus*
by Richard MS Irwin, **CC BY-SA 4.0**, which permits use in the app with credit (shown on the
hymn's page). *It Is Well with My Soul* is a CC0 instrumental setting.
